# Introduction

I've been designing and building digital products for 15 years, working across many different sectors with companies of varying sizes.

# Process

I embed within product teams—from small delivery groups to senior leadership—to help drive projects forward, shaping the user experience, maintaining key stakeholder relationships, and working closely with engineers to deliver high-quality outcomes. My client history includes _Carhartt WIP_, _Highsnobiety_, _Fotografiska_, _Superhuman (Grammarly)_, _SoundCloud_, and _DIE ZEIT_.

# Principles

We exist in a world in which our lives and our work are being reshaped by technology. As technologists, we are the custodians of that shift. While I'm open and generally excited about these changes, I think it's important to also be critical of them. What do we want this world to look like?
