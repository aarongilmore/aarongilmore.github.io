### Introduction & Context
Carhartt WIP has always occupied a distinctive place across music, skateboarding, art and fashion, but its digital products didn’t yet reflect the same clarity or cultural relevance.

We joined as a long-term design partner to rethink how the brand showed up across its app, consumer shop and wholesale platform—creating a more connected experience that Carhartt WIP’s internal teams could own and continue to evolve.

### My Role
I’ve been embedded within Carhartt WIP’s product team since 2024, working across its consumer, mobile, and wholesale experiences.

My work on the B2C platform has covered core journeys such as purchasing, discovery, alongside editorial modules and supporting pages including sustainability, Carhartt WIP Radio, and the editorial archive. 

Within the app, I focused primarily on the relationship between content and commerce—exploring how editorial stories could lead naturally into product discovery.

Since 2025, I’ve also led the design of the B2B marketing hub, shaping the product from its core structure and workflows through to the finer details of the experience. The platform gives retail partners access to the content and tools they need to present Carhartt WIP consistently across their own channels.

### Design Approach
We designed a connected ecosystem spanning the app, B2C shop and B2B platform, with shared patterns and a flexible foundation that Carhartt WIP’s teams could manage and evolve themselves.

Working as part of an embedded team means my role often extends beyond design. I take an active part in shaping priorities, aligning stakeholders and turning broad requirements into clear, achievable steps.

I work closely with product managers and engineers throughout the process, sharing work early and using regular feedback to keep projects moving. This helps maintain momentum across parallel work streams while ensuring decisions remain grounded in user needs, technical constraints and the wider product strategy.

### Takeaways
The work has resulted in a modern, scalable website and app, supported by strong customer satisfaction and conversion rates. More importantly, the shared design system has created a clear foundation for future projects—allowing new features and experiences to be developed more quickly without losing consistency across the wider ecosystem.
