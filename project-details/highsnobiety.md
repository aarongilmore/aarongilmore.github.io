### Introduction & Context

Highsnobiety had spent close to two decades building its voice across fashion, music, art and culture. We joined the team to explore how that editorial point of view could translate into a mobile product for its most engaged readers.

Rather than recreating the website as an app, we worked together to define what the format could offer on its own—bringing editorial discovery, saved content and shopping into a more direct and personal experience.

### My Role

I focused on designing the ecommerce experience, exploring how shopping could sit naturally alongside Highsnobiety’s editorial voice rather than feeling like a separate part of the product.

I led a series of customer interviews to better understand people’s behaviours and needs, using those insights to shape the core journeys. I also worked on a more story-driven experience that could support Highsnobiety’s editorial approach and create stronger connections between content and commerce.

Throughout the project, I worked closely with key stakeholders across the business—from product owners through to the CEO—sharing work regularly and building alignment around the direction of the product.

### Design Approach

The central challenge was bringing editorial and ecommerce together without either feeling compromised. Rather than treating content and shopping as separate experiences, we explored how stories could become a natural entry point into product discovery and purchase.

This meant designing sales journeys that began with context and inspiration rather than a conventional product grid. Products could be introduced through the people, ideas and culture around them, creating a new way to shop that still felt recognisably Highsnobiety.

The design itself was kept deliberately simple and modular. A clear, scalable system gave the editorial team room to tell richer stories while allowing content and commerce to connect in different ways as the platform evolved.

### Takeaways

The project reinforced that editorial can be more than a layer applied to an ecommerce experience—it can shape the entire journey. By beginning with stories rather than products, we created more considered paths into commerce that reflected how Highsnobiety’s audience already discovered brands and ideas.

It also showed the importance of keeping the underlying system simple. The more flexible and scalable the foundation became, the more freedom the editorial team had to experiment without losing consistency or making the shopping experience harder to understand.
