### Introduction & Context
Towards the end of 2025, we joined Superhuman as a design partner to help shape the vision for its mobile ecosystem. With no ramp-up time, we moved quickly from early ideation and stakeholder interviews into prototyping and delivery, producing a substantial body of work in just three months.

### My Role
The project began across two parallel tracks: exploring ideas without the constraints of a fixed brief, and developing key personas through interviews with stakeholders across Superhuman. These personas evolved into three primary user journeys.

My focus was Superhuman Mail, where I helped translate the wider product vision into a clear and considered mobile experience. Alongside this, I explored how the Grammarly keyboard could evolve into Superhuman Go—a proactive AI assistant designed to offer contextual support across the apps and websites people already use. This became a focused side project, developed in close collaboration with Superhuman’s iOS tech lead to better understand the technical constraints and opportunities of bringing that experience to mobile.

### Design Approach
Given the scope and compressed timeline, I worked in short, focused cycles—developing ideas, sharing tangible work and refining the direction through regular feedback.
Maintaining momentum was essential. By putting new work in front of the client frequently, I was able to test assumptions early, build alignment and move the product forward with confidence.

### Takeaways
The AI landscape has shifted significantly since this work began, and some of the thinking may already be evolving alongside it. That pace of change was one of the project’s most valuable lessons.

I’m proud of the depth and volume of work delivered in three months. The project also strengthened my understanding of complex product systems and how to navigate them at speed without losing sight of the user experience.
