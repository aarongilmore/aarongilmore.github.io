### Introduction & Context

OS/ began as a set of internal tools created by agency founders who wanted a clearer view of project profitability and the wider health of their businesses. We worked with the team as the product evolved from something used internally into a comprehensive software suite for the wider market.

The challenge was to make complex financial information feel approachable while creating a flexible design foundation that could grow alongside the product.

### My Role

I was responsible for building and maintaining a complex design system spanning four key platforms within the suite. I designed reference screens and data visualisations that established how the system should work in practice, bringing the clarity and utility of a mature SaaS product to a large amount of financial and operational information.

Alongside the hands-on design work, I managed the day-to-day client relationship—turning feedback and evolving requirements into clear priorities for the team. I also supported a junior designer throughout the project, providing direction and feedback while helping them take ownership of their work.

### Design Approach

We approached the suite as one connected system rather than four separate products. Shared foundations created consistency across the platforms, while flexible patterns allowed each one to respond to its own workflows and types of information.

Reference screens were an important part of the process. By applying the system to realistic, data-heavy views, we could test hierarchy, density and interaction early rather than resolving components in isolation. Data visualisations were designed with the same principles: making complex information easier to compare and understand without removing the detail people needed to make decisions.

The interface was kept purposeful and direct, with the familiar clarity of a SaaS product. This allowed the design to recede when necessary, giving workflows and information priority while creating a foundation that could continue to scale as the suite developed.

### Takeaways

The project reinforced that maintaining a design system is as much about decision-making and communication as it is about components. Working across four platforms meant continually deciding what should be shared, where variation was necessary and how new requirements could strengthen the wider system rather than introduce exceptions.

It also developed my ability to lead across different levels of a project: maintaining a close client relationship, keeping the work moving and supporting another designer while remaining involved in the detail. The result was a more coherent suite and a design foundation capable of accommodating increasingly complex workflows and information.
